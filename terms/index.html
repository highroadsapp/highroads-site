2. Safety comes first

Highroads is a game about the roads you drive. It must never make your driving less safe.

  • Never use the app while driving. Don't look at, tap or set up the app while the car is moving. Start a trip before you set off and end it once you've stopped somewhere safe, or let a passenger do it.
  • If your phone is in the car, put it in a fixed mount that doesn't block your view, and only if that's legal where you are. Australian road rules on phone use are strict and the penalties are heavy. Know the rules in your state.
  • Obey every road rule: speed limits, signs, signals, no-entry and one-way streets, school zones, and the directions of police and traffic controllers.
  • Never drive recklessly, or go out of your way unsafely, to win a crown, clear a block, keep a streak or climb a leaderboard. Don't drive tired, impaired, or in conditions you're not comfortable with because of the game.
  • Don't enter private property, closed roads or places you're not allowed to go to chase a street. Highroads counts public streets only.
  • The app's map, street names and road data come from OpenStreetMap and may be wrong or out of date. Always follow the actual road, signs and conditions, not the app. Highroads is not a navigation app.

We may suspend or close an account where we reasonably believe the game is being played unsafely (for example, trips that show dangerous speeds).

3. Fair play

To keep the game fair for everyone, you agree not to:

  • fake, edit, replay or share GPS data or trips, or use emulators, spoofing apps or anything else that makes trips look like they happened when they didn't;
  • drive the same trip under several accounts, or use someone else's account;
  • try to get around the game's limits (for example on speed, trip length or uploads), or probe, attack or overload our servers;
  • choose a driver name, plate, crew name or note that is offensive, impersonates someone, or breaks the law;
  • harass, threaten or try to find or follow another player, including by using crowns, leaderboards, friends or crews to work out where someone lives or drives.

Our server checks trips for things like impossible speeds and jumps, and may not count a trip that fails them. If we find cheating, we may remove the trips, crowns, badges or leaderboard places it affected, and suspend or close the account. If you think we've got it wrong, contact us at [support email].

  • Things you choose to share, such as share cards and posters, are shared by you. Think about what's in them before you post them.
  • You keep the rights in anything you submit (like feedback notes), and you let us use it to run and improve Highroads.

5. Friends and crews

  • Friend requests and crew invites are up to you to accept. You can remove a friend or leave a crew at any time.
  • Don't use friends or crews to pressure anyone into driving, or into driving unsafely.
