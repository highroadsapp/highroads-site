How it plays

Clear the fog

Start a trip and drive. The roads you take light up and the fog clears around them, so your map shows exactly where you've been.

Loop the blocks

Drive all the way round a block and the whole block clears. Circle Melbourne's landmarks, from the MCG to the Shrine, to discover them.

JAZ88 holds Park St · 4 drives

Take the crown

Drive a street more than anyone else in 30 days and its crown is yours. Climb your suburb's leaderboard each week, solo or with your crew.

Private by design

Location is personal. Highroads is built so other players see your game, never your movements.

  • Your trip lines are yours. No other player ever sees where or when you drove.
  • Home stays hidden. The first and last 200 m of every trip never count publicly, and your plate never shows on streets near where your trips start and end.
  • Only what you'd put on a scoreboard. Other players see a driver name, a made-up game plate, kilometres and crowns.
  • Stored in Sydney. Download everything, or delete your account, any time in the app.
