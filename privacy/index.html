Highroads Privacy Policy

Draft, not yet in force. Written in plain English for Holdt Media to review. Have it checked by a lawyer before publishing. Items in [square brackets] need filling in or a decision.

Last updated: [date]

Highroads is a driving game: your phone records the roads you drive, clears the fog off them on your map, and turns them into scores, leaderboards and street crowns. That means we handle location data, which is personal and sensitive. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, where it's kept, and how to download or delete it.

Highroads is made by Holdt Media Pty Ltd (ABN [ABN]), an Australian company ("we", "us"). Questions or requests: [privacy contact email].

The short version

  • We only record your location while a trip is recording. You start and end every trip.
  • Your trip lines are private. No other player can ever see where you drove, when, or where you started or finished.
  • The ends of every trip are hidden. The first and last 200 m of every trip are never used for anything other players see, and other players never see your plate on streets near where your trips usually start or end.
  • Other players only see your driver name, game plate, kilometres, crowns and drive counts.
  • Your data is stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • You can download all your data or delete your account at any time, in the app.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address, to log you in with a one-time code. We never ask players for a password (one account used only by Apple's App Review team has one).

Your profile (you choose all of it)

  • A driver name and a made-up game plate (not your real registration), both shown to other players.
  • Your car make and model and an optional nickname for it.
  • An optional home suburb. This is never shown to other players.

Your trips

  • While a trip is recording: your phone's GPS location (latitude, longitude and time) every few seconds, and how accurate each reading is. Inaccurate readings are thrown away on your phone.
  • The trip matched to the road map ("snapped route"), worked out on your phone.
  • What the trip scored: kilometres, new roads, streets driven, crowns and your daily streak.

Recording with the screen locked (in versions of the app that support it)

  • If you allow "Always" location access, Highroads keeps recording only while a trip you started is recording, and stops when you end it. iOS shows a location indicator while it does.

Crews

  • Which crew you're in, and when you joined.

Technical information

  • Our database host keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for security and to fix problems.

We don't collect your contacts, photos, microphone, or location when no trip is recording.

2. How we use it

  • To show your map: clearing fog, drawing your roads, landmarks and blocks.
  • To score your trips on our server: new road kilometres per suburb, street passes, crowns, crews and streaks. Scoring happens on the server so nobody can cheat by editing the app.
  • To stop cheating: readings that would mean driving faster than 160 km/h, impossible jumps, and trips with under 2 minutes of driving don't count.
  • To run leaderboards and crews.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to profile you.

3. What other players can see

| Other players can see | Other players never see | | --- | --- | | Your driver name and game plate | Your trip lines or snapped routes | | Your new road km on suburb leaderboards (this week and all time) | When you drove | | How many crowns you hold, and on which streets | Where your trips start or end | | Your plate and drive count on a crowned street (outside your privacy zones) | Your email address or home suburb | | Your crew's name, size, km and crowns | Your car details |

Privacy zones

  • Trip ends: the first and last 200 m of every trip are cut off before anything public is worked out.
  • Plates near your trip ends: on any street within 300 m of where one of your trips started or ended, your crown shows as a plain crown, with no plate and no drive count.

Crews: people in your crew can see the other members' driver names and plates. Only members see the crew's invite code.

Test players: before launch, the leaderboards may include made-up "Test Rival" players, clearly marked as test data.

4. Where it's stored, and who helps us

  • Supabase stores your account, profile, trips and scores in its Sydney, Australia region, on Amazon Web Services. Supabase also sends the login code emails. [If a separate email provider is set up later (for example Resend or Postmark), name it here.]
  • Map display: the map itself is drawn by Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps (Android), which load map images for the area on screen, as any maps app does.
  • Road data: to snap trips to roads, the app downloads road shapes from OpenStreetMap. [Today these come from public OpenStreetMap "Overpass" servers run by others in Germany and Russia, which see your IP address and the roughly 2 km square of road data requested. Before launch we plan to serve road data from our own Supabase database in Sydney instead; update this paragraph when that's done.]

Some of these companies may handle data outside Australia. We only use them to run Highroads.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and trips until you delete them. When you delete your account, everything is deleted from our live database straight away. Copies in our host's routine backups are overwritten within [number] days.

6. Download or delete your data

  • Download my data: Settings → Download my data gives you a file with your account, profile, crew and every trip (including all GPS points and scores).
  • Delete account: Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your login, profile, game plate, every trip and score, your crowns, and your crew membership, from your phone and our servers. There's no undo.
  • You can also email [privacy contact email] to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion.

7. Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules (row-level security) mean each player can only read their own trips and profile; public features only ever use the trimmed, scored results described above. Login uses one-time email codes instead of passwords.

8. Age and safe driving

Highroads is for licensed drivers, and is not meant for anyone under [16]. Never use your phone while driving. Start a trip before you set off and end it once you've stopped.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.

10. Questions and complaints

Contact [privacy contact email]. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
