How to test Highroads

Thanks for testing! Highroads turns your everyday driving into a game. This page is everything you need. It takes 3 minutes to read.

Safety first, every time

  • Set up before you drive: open Highroads and tap Start trip while you're parked.
  • Phone in a cradle, hands off. Never touch the phone while the car is moving, even at lights. Highroads keeps recording with the screen locked, and nothing in it needs a tap while you drive.
  • Drive normally. There are no points for speed, and no reason to take risks or detour for the game. Normal road rules always come first.
  • A passenger is welcome to watch the map and play along.

The basics

  1. Start trip before you set off, End trip when you've parked. Forgot to start? No worries, just start next time.
  2. The fog: your map starts covered. Every road you drive glows amber and clears around it.
  3. Blocks: drive all the way round a block (with no street inside) and the whole block clears.
  4. Crowns: drive a street more than anyone else over 30 days and its crown is yours.
  5. Kings: each week (Monday to Sunday), whoever drives the most different roads in a suburb leads it, and on Monday becomes its King for the week.
  6. After each trip, the trip summary shows new streets, blocks and crowns. It can take a minute to fill in.

What to try over the next two weeks

  • Your normal drives: work, shops, school runs. This is the most useful thing you can do.
  • One drive somewhere new (a suburb you don't usually go to).
  • Loop a block on purpose, and see it clear.
  • Lock your screen during a trip and check the whole route was recorded.
  • Look at Profile: your suburbs passport, badges and streak.
  • Leaderboard: your suburb's board and your weekly league. Where do you rank?
  • Add a friend (Profile → Friends) and compare territory: Layers button on the map → Mine / Friends / Everyone.
  • Design your helmet (Profile → Customise).
  • Share a trip card or a crown.
  • When parked: long-press a road that's wrong on the map (closed, private, missing) to report it.

Telling us what happened

  • Settings → Send feedback: write what happened and add a screenshot. This is the best way.
  • Shake your phone (when parked) to send feedback from any screen.
  • TestFlight: take a screenshot, then tap Share Beta Feedback.
  • Something broke? Tell us roughly when, where (suburb is fine) and what you expected. A screenshot helps most.

Useful things to tell us: trips that didn't record or recorded oddly (straight lines, missing bits), a summary that never filled in, a crown or King you think is wrong, anything confusing, and what you enjoyed.

Good to know

  • Privacy: your trips and routes are never shown to other players. They see your plate on the streets and suburbs you hold. You can download or delete everything in Settings.
  • Battery: Highroads uses GPS only while a trip records. Plugging in on long drives helps.
  • It's a beta: things will change and occasionally break, and scores may be reset before launch.
  • Updates arrive through TestFlight. Keep automatic updates on.
